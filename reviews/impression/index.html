Impression offers competitive pricing with a focus on delivering value through effective SEO and PPC strategies. Clients report substantial ROI, including a 119% revenue increase for one client. Their flexibility and responsiveness to budget changes enhance overall satisfaction.

  • Areas for Improvement: Report Digestibility. One client mentioned that Impression's detailed analysis and reporting could be difficult to digest for non-PPC experts. They worked with Impression to structure monthly reports and commentary for clearer communication.
  • Clear Communication and Responsiveness. Clients consistently praised Impression's clear and open communication, quick responsiveness, and ability to manage expectations. They are described as reliable, skilled, and proactive in addressing client needs.
  • Commitment to Continuous Improvement. Clients appreciated Impression's commitment to continuous improvement, exploring new opportunities and providing the best possible online visibility. They did not settle after achieving initial objectives.
  • Significant Lead Generation and Growth. Impression has driven Year on Year growth of +66% (2021 vs 2020) and +71% (2022 vs 2021) in leads for Stanhope Insurance, maintaining a sustainable CPA. This growth has enabled the company to expand staff and branch into new product areas.
