NP Digital offers competitive pricing that clients find valuable, with many noting it fits their budgets. Specific project costs range from $8,000 to $200,000, depending on client size and scope. Overall, clients appreciate timely delivery and effective results, indicating strong value for cost.

  • Improvements in Technical SEO. NP Digital has helped clients address technical SEO issues such as site structure and duplicate content, leading to enhanced website performance and search rankings.
  • High Cost for Smaller Clients. Some clients have noted that NP Digital’s services are costly, particularly for smaller businesses. There is feedback suggesting that smaller companies might not receive the same level of attention as larger accounts.
  • Strong Project Management and Communication. Clients appreciate NP Digital’s structured project management and clear communication. The team is praised for meeting deadlines, being responsive, and providing regular updates and reports, facilitating a smooth workflow and effective collaboration.
  • Effective Use of Advanced Tools. Clients appreciate NP Digital’s use of advanced tools and technologies that smaller agencies may not afford, providing valuable insights and actions to drive progress.
