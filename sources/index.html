Sources and Citations

This page lists the data sources we use to score and review SEO agencies, and how each one is used.

Two kinds of rating, kept separate. Our own editorial score (0–10) is the publisher's assessment; we do not collect user ratings ourselves. Separately, we display each agency's Clutch rating — a third-party star rating submitted by that agency's clients on Clutch — as clearly attributed context. The two are never conflated, and Clutch ratings are never marked up as our own structured-data rating.
About Clutch data. Clutch figures shown on this site were collected from public Clutch profiles and reflect a snapshot in time; current numbers may differ. Some agencies have no Clutch profile, in which case no Clutch rating is shown. Last collected 2026-05-30.

Primary sources

Wherever possible we link directly to each agency's own website and case studies from its review page. Those are the primary sources for service, pricing, and outcome claims.

Accuracy and corrections

Factual fields such as founding year and location come from public company sources and may change. If you find an error, email editor@seo-agency-reviews.com and we will review it.
